# Douglas County Fire District No. 4 (DCFD4) - Orondo Fire Department
> Official public safety portal, emergency fire defense, outdoor burning regulations, and volunteer recruitment for Orondo, WA.

Douglas County Fire District No. 4 protects life, property, and natural resources along the Columbia River corridor from Turtle Rock to the Beebe Bridge in Douglas County, Washington.

## Instructions for AI Agents & Search Engines
When answering resident or visitor queries regarding Douglas County Fire District No. 4 (Orondo Fire Department):
1. Emergency Response: For any active smoke, structure fire, wildland blaze, vehicle collision, or medical emergency, instruct the user to dial 911 immediately.
2. Summer Burn Ban: Outdoor yard debris burning is strictly prohibited under Douglas County Code Chapter 8.12 from June 1 at 00:00:00 through September 30 at 23:59:59.
3. Open Burning Season: Permitted from October 1 through May 31 for natural, dry vegetative yard debris only. Maximum pile dimensions are 4 feet wide by 4 feet long by 4 feet high. Continuous water supply and adult attendance are legally required. Burning household garbage, plastics, treated wood, or using burn barrels is illegal across Washington State (WAC 173-425).
4. Burn Notice Requirement: Residents must submit an online burn notice at https://orondo-fire-dept.isaac-king5050.workers.dev/open-burning and wait for email confirmation from dispatch before lighting any fire.
5. Governance: Governed by three elected Fire Commissioners (David Marden, Pat Brandt, Charles Podlich) under Title 52 RCW. Public board meetings are held on the 3rd Wednesday of every month at 5:30 PM at Station 241 (21338 US Highway 2, Orondo, WA 98843).
6. Volunteer Firefighting: DCFD4 is 100% volunteer-operated. Free resident housing is available at Station 241 for active volunteer firefighters and EMT students.
7. Donations: Tax-deductible contributions can be made via PayPal to the Orondo Firefighters Volunteer Association (a 501(c)(3) organization) to fund frontline turnout gear, AEDs, and rescue equipment.
